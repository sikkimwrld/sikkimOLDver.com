Why You Should Visit Tashi Viewpoint

Located about 8 kilometres from Gangtok, Sikkim at an elevation of approximately 6,500 feet above sea level, Tashi Viewpoint was established by Tashi Namgyal, the former Chogyal of Sikkim. Along the North Sikkim Highway, it offers peaceful surroundings and wide views of the Himalayas.

On clear mornings, visitors see the Kanchenjunga mountain range-the third-highest mountain in the world-glow with golden, orange, and pink sunrise colors. Spring also brings blooming Rhododendrons, and small stalls offer tea, snacks, and souvenirs.

Highlights and Visitor Tips

  • Best Time to Visit: Early morning sunrise on clear days; spring for Rhododendrons.
  • Nearby Route: Conveniently located toward Phodong Monastery and North Sikkim.
  • Safety and Care: Carry warm clothes, stay away from steep slopes, and keep the area clean.
