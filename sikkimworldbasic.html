Bhim Falls

Bhim Falls

Bhim Falls (also known as Bheem Nala Falls) is a scenic multi-stage waterfall near Lachung on the road to Yumthang Valley, surrounded by green Himalayan hills and cool mountain air.

"The roar of the waterfall from the wooden bridge is incredible, with cool mist drifting across the road. A refreshing stop on the drive toward Lachung."

- Meera Nair


Tashi Viewpoint

Tashi Viewpoint

Located about 8 km from Gangtok at an elevation of around 6,500 feet, Tashi Viewpoint offers peaceful surroundings and sunrise views over the Kanchenjunga mountain range.

"Watching the first morning sunlight turn Mount Kanchenjunga golden was unforgettable. Crisp mountain air and hot tea right nearby."

- Aarav Sharma


Baba H.S. Temple

Baba H.S. Temple

Also known as Baba Mandir, this high-altitude shrine near Nathu La Pass honors Indian Army soldier Harbhajan Singh of the 23 Punjab Regiment amidst dramatic mountain scenery.

"A deeply peaceful and inspiring high-altitude visit near Nathu La. Everything is spotless and surrounded by snow-dusted peaks."

- Rohan Verma


Saramsa Garden

Saramsa Garden

Established in 1922 near Ranipool, Saramsa Garden is a terraced botanical garden known for Himalayan orchids, seasonal flowers, fernries, and calm walking paths.

"Loved walking through the orchid greenhouses and quiet terraced lawns. A calm, green break just a short drive outside Gangtok."

- Priya Das
