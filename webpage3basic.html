Why You Should Visit Baba H.S. Temple

The Baba Harbhajan Singh Temple, popularly known as Baba Mandir, is a high-altitude shrine in East Sikkim near Nathu La Pass dedicated to Harbhajan Singh, a soldier of the 23 Punjab Regiment who went missing in October 1968 in the mountains.

Both the original Old Baba Mandir and the newer Baba Mandir attract pilgrims, soldiers, and visitors along the scenic route near Nathu La, Tsomgo Lake, and Nathang Valley.

Highlights and Visitor Tips

  • Permits and Rules: Carry required permits for the border region and follow local photography rules.
  • Weather and Care: Wear heavy warm clothing for high altitude and keep the fragile mountains clean.
